How to use it
  • Fill one in when you notice a change in how you feel, as soon as you can while it’s fresh.
  • The five parts affect each other: what you think changes how you feel and what you do, and what you do changes how things go next.
  • Emotions are usually one word, like sad, angry or anxious. Thoughts are sentences, pictures in your mind or memories.
  • Physical feelings are what you notice in your body. Behaviours are what you did, avoided, or felt like doing.
  • Rate each emotion from 0 (not at all) to 100% (as strong as it gets).
  • Before each session, open Session summary to share your records.
